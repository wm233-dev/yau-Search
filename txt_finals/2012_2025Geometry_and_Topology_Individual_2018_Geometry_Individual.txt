S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contests 2018
Oral Exams in Geometry and Topology
Individual (4 problems)
1. Is the surface of genus 3 a covering space of the surface of genus 2. Is the surface
of genus 2 a covering space of the surface of genus 3. If so, then exhibit an example of
such a covering.
2. Consider a smooth map f : S2n−1 →Sn with n ≥2. Let ν be a volume form on Sn
with volume 1.
a). Show that f ∗ν is exact.
b). Write f ∗ν = dα. Show that the integral
Z
S2n−1 α ∧f ∗ν
is independent of the choice of α.
c). Show that the integral above is actually an invariant of the homotopy class of f.
d). Show that the integral is 0 if n is odd.
e). Calculate this integral if f is the Hopf map (z0, z1) →[z0, z1].
3. (D. Hilbert) There does not exist a complete regular surface in R3 whose Gaussian
curvature is a negative constant K0.
4. Let CP 2 denote the complex projective plane with the opposite orientation.
a). Show that S2×S2 and CP 2#CP 2 have the same cohomology group but diﬀerent
cohomology ring.
b). Show that (S2 × S2)#CP 2 and CP 2#2CP 2 have the same cohomology.
