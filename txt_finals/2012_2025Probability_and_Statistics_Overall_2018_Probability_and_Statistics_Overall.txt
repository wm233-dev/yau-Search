Yau Mathematical Competition 2018
Probability and Statistics Overall
Problem 1 (Probability) Let X be a real valued random variable such that
Ef(X +1) = E(X f(X))
for all smooth function f with compact support.
(1) Show that X has a rapidly vanishing positive tail probability, i.e.,
P{X > N} ≤1
N!.
(2) Use (1) or otherwise to show that X has the standard Poisson distribution P(1).
