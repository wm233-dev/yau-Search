S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contests 2016
Oral Exam of Computational and Applied Math
1. Consider n scalars: a(1), a(2), . . . , a(n). Our goal is to ﬁnd the max partial sum,
that is, ﬁnd two indices p and q, where p ≤q, such that the partial sum, a(p : q) =
a(p) + a(p + 1) + · · · + a(q), is maximized. Develop an algorithm that ﬁnds p, q, and
a(p : q) by accessing each a(i) only once. For solution existence, assume that not all
scalars are negative. Please provide a convincing oral proof to the correctness of your
algorithm
