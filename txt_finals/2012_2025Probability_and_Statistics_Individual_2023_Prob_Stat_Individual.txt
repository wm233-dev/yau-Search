Yau College Math Competition 2023
Final Probability and Statistics
Individual Exam Problems (June 10-11, 2023)
Choose 3 of the following 4 problems.
Problem 1.
Suppose that Yn ∼Poisson(n) is a Poisson random variable with
parameter n ∈N∗= {1, 2, · · · }.
(1) Calculate E(Yn −n)+, where x+ = max{x, 0}.
(2) Prove that E(Yn−n)+
√n
converges to EN+ as n →∞, where N ∼N(0, 1)
(standard normal).
(3) Use the above results to derive Stirling’s formula for the factorial n!.
Problem 2. For any random variables ξ and η on the same probability space taking
values in N∗= {1, 2, · · · }, assume that P(ξ is divisible by r) = P(η is divisible by r),
∀r ∈N∗. Prove or disprove that ξ
d= η (i.e., ξ and η have the same distribution).
Problem 3. Fix some integer k ≥2. For any n ≥1, let {Xn,i : i = 1, 2, · · · , k} be
i.i.d. uniform random variables taking values in {1, 2, · · · , n}. Let Zn = gcd{Xn,i :
i = 1, 2, · · · , k} be the greatest common divisor of {Xn,i : i = 1, 2, · · · , k}.
(1) Prove that Zn converges in law to some limit Z with P(Z = r) ∝r−k, ∀r ∈
N∗.
(2) Choose k numbers independently and uniformly from {1, 2, · · · , n}. Let Pn(k)
be the probability that these numbers are relatively prime. Find limn→∞Pn(k).
Problem 4.
Let X1, . . . , Xn be independent observations.
Assume that Xi ∼
Poisson(iλ), where λ is an unkonwn parameter.
(1) Find an unbiased estimator of the parameter λ. Is your estimator the best
unbiased estimator in terms of minimizing the mean squared error?
(2) Is your estimator asymptotically normal?
