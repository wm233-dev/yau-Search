Yau College Math Competition 2020 Final Probability and Statistics
Overal Exam Problem Set (Saturday, October 24, 2020)
PROBLEM 1. Let {Xn} be a sequence of independent, identically dis-
tributed random variables taking values in N, the set of positive integers.
Deﬁne
Rn = Card{X1, · · · , Xn},
i.e., Rn is the number of distinct integers in the set {X1, · · · , Xn}. Suppose
that E[X1] < ∞. Prove that Rn/√n →0 in probability.
PROBLEM 2. Consider the mixture experiment whose components are
E1 and E2, taken with equal probabilities and each with the sample space
Ω= {0, 1, 2, ...}. It is postulated that
(1) the outcome of E1 follows the Binomial model Binomial(n, θ) with
the total number of trials n > 0 and the unknown probability of
success parameter θ ∈[0, 1], and
(2) the outcome of E2 follows the Negative-Binomial model Binomial(r, θ)
with the target for number of successful trials r > 0 and the un-
known probability of success parameter θ ∈[0, 1].
Let H be the observed index to the experiment that is actually conducted,
and let X denote the observed outcome of the conducted experiment.
Find a minimal sufﬁcient statistic for θ and prove your claim.
