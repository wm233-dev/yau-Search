Problem 1. Let G be a finite group, H ≤G a subgroup, N = NG(H) its normalizer and
N′ = NG(N) the normalizer of its normalizer. Assume that the order |H| of H is coprime
to the index [N : H]. Prove that N = N′.
Problem 2. Let F be a field. A Zariski closed subset Y of ¯F n is F-closed if the ideal
I corresponding to Y is defined over F. A subset of ¯F n is F-open if its complement is
F-closed. Do the F-open sets always form a topology on ¯F n?

the relations
aibj + bjai = δij1.
Classify all simple A-modules.
(b) What if the relations are replaced by these
aibj −bjai = δij1 ?
