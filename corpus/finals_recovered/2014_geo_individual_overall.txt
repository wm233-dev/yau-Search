Geometry and Topology
Oral Exam (Individual)
1. How many S^1 bundles on S^2 are there?
2. How many S^2 bundles on S^2 are there?
3. State and prove the maximal diameter theorem.
4. There is no non-constant holomorphic map from a compact Riemann surface of genus g to a compact Riemann surface of genus h, where g < h.
5. There is at least one point p in a closed surface, s.t., K(p) > 0.
