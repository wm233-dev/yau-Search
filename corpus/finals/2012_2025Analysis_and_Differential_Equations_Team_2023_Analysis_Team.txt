Please solve two from the following three problems.
1. Given two lattices Γ1 = Z · ω1,1 + Z · ω1,2 and Γ2 = Z · ω2,1 + Z · ω2,2 in the complex
plane C having full rank. Let f : C →C be an entire function inducing a holomorphic
map between two elliptic curves C/Γ1 and C/Γ2, i.e.,
f(z) = f(z + γ)
(mod Γ2)
(∀z ∈C, γ ∈Γ1).
Show that f(z) = a·z+b for any z ∈C, where a, b are some complex numbers satisfying
a · Γ1 ⊂Γ2, b ∈Γ2.
2. Let T be a bounded self-adjoint operator, prove that the spectrum of T is not empty.
If T is self-adjoint but unbounded, is the spectrum of T necessarily non-empty? Prove
your conclusion.
3. Let {fn}n be a bounded sequence in L2(R3), and let {gn}n be a sequence of Schwartz
functions.
Assume,
(0.1)
(−∆+ |x|2)gn = fn.
Prove that
• {gn}n is a bounded sequence in L2(R3).
• One can ﬁnd a subsequence {gnk}k, which converges in L2(R3).
