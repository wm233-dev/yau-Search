2017 Oral Exam: Probability and Statistics
Individual
Problem 1. Let X be a random variable with ﬁnite variance. Denote by
m, µ, σ the median, mean and standard deviation of X:
m := inf{c : P[X ≤c] ≥1/2},
µ = E[X],
σ2 = E[(X −µ)2].
Show that |m −µ| ≤σ.
Problem 2. Let (Xn)n≥1 be a sequence of non-negative random variables.
Let (Fn)n≥1 be a ﬁltration (i.e. a sequence of increasing σ-algebras). Assume
that
E[Xn | Fn] →0,
in probability.
Show that
Xn →0,
in probability.
Is it true reversely? If yes, prove it; if not, give a counterexample.
Problem 3. Let X1, ..., Xn be independent random variables following com-
mon Poisson distribution with mean λ. Let η = e−λ. Does there exist a
uniformly unbiased minimum variance estimator UMVUE of η? (Recall that
an estimator is UMVUE if it is unbaised estimator and has smallest variance
among all unbiased estimators.) If yes, ﬁnd it; if no, prove it.
