Problem 2
Let G be a ﬁnite group acting on the polynomial ring R = k[x1, · · · , xn] with
n variables x1, · · · , xn. Let S := {f ∈R | g · f = f, ∀g ∈G} be the subring of
invariants. Prove that S is a ﬁnitely generated k-algebra.
Problem 3
Let k be any ﬁeld. Let R = k[[t]] be the ring of formal power series over k. Let
M be a ﬁnitely generated free R-module. Let v1, · · · , vn ∈M, and denote their
images in M/tM by ¯v1, · · · , ¯vn. Assume that {¯v1, · · · , ¯vn} is a basis of the vector
space M/tM over k. Prove that {v1, · · · , vn} is an R-basis of the module M.
Remark. This is a special case of Nakayama’s lemma.
