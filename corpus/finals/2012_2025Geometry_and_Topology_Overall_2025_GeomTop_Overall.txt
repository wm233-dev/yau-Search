S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contests 2025
Oral Exams in Geometry and Topology
All-round (Solve 1 out of 2 problems)
1. Let Sn be the unit n-sphere and TSn be its tangent bundle.
(1) Show that TSn × R is a trivial bundle over Sn.
(2) If n = 2m is even, show that TS2m is not a trivial bundle.
2. Let ω be a volume form on S2. For each map f : S3 →S2, the form f ∗ω is a closed
2-form, so we can write f ∗ω = dα for some 1-form α. The number
H(f) =
Z
S3 α ∧dα
is called the Hopf invariant of f.
(1) Prove that H(f) does not depend on the choice of α.
(2) Prove that homotopic maps have the same Hopf invariant.
