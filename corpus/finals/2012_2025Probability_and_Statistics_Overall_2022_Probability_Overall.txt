Individual Overall Exam Problems (Aug. 20-21, 2022)
Problem 1. Let X and Y be independent identically distributed random variables
with mean 0 and variance 1. Let ϕ(t) be their common characteristic function, and
suppose that X + Y and X −Y are independent.
(1) Deduce a relation between ϕ(2t) and ϕ(±t).
(2) Show that X and Y are N(0, 1) random variables.
