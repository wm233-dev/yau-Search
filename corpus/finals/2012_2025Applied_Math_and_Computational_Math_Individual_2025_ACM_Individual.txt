Yau 2025 Applied Math – Individual Contest
1. Consider the following boundary value problem for the stationary Schr¨odinger equation:
−∆u(x) + V (x)u(x) = 0
in Ω;
∂νu(x) = ψ(x)
on ∂Ω,
(1)
where V ∈L2(Ω), ν denotes the exterior unit normal vector to ∂Ω, and ψ ∈H−1/2(∂Ω). It is assumed
that 0 is not a Neumann eigenvalue of the operator −∆+V in Ω, ensuring the boundary value problem
(1) is well-posed with a unique solution u ∈H1(Ω) for any ψ. For fixed ψ, define the boundary dataset:
Mψ := u|∂Ω∈H1/2(∂Ω),
(2)
where u solves (1). The inverse problem is to recover V from Mψ with a given ψ, formulated as the
operator equation:
F(V ) = Mψ,
(3)
where F is defined via (1).
(a) Prove that F in (3) is Fr´echet differentiable and derive its derivative.
(b) Suppose V ∈Span{V1(x), . . . , VN(x)} for given functions Vj. Sketch a Newton-type method for
solving (3) using (a).
