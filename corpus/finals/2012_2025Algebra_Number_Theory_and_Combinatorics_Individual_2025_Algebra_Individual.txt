Algebra, Number theory and Combinatorics
Individual Contest
Question 1. Let Mn be the space of real n×n matrices with euclidean topology.
Let G = GLn(R) act on Mn by conjugation. For a diagonal matrix D ∈Mn,
denote by GD the G-orbit of D. Show that GD is closed in Mn.
Question 2. Let A be the abelian category of ﬁnite abelian groups. Show that
A has neither enough projective objects nor enough injective objects.
Question 3. Let G be a group of cardinality n. Prove that G has at most
nlog2 n automorphisms.
