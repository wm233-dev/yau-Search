S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contests 2019
Geometry and Topology
Team (5 problems)
1) Is TS2 diﬀeomorphic to S2 × R2? Verify your answer. Here TS2 is the total space of the tangent
bundle of S2.
2) Solve the problem which Russell Crowe assigns to his students in the movie “A beautiful mind” (2001):
V = {F : R3\X →R3 s.t. ∇× F = 0}
W = {F = ∇g}
dim (V/W) =?
First give the general answer for any closed X ⊂R3, and then specialize it to (a) X = {x = y = z = 0},
(b) X = {x = y = 0} and (c) X = {x = 0}.
3) Let T 2 = S1 × S1 be the 2-torus with the standard orientation, and let F : T 2 →T 2 be a smooth
map of degree 1 such that F ◦F = Id and F has no ﬁxed points.
Prove that the induced map
F ∗: H1(T 2) →H1(T 2) is the identity.
4) Let U(n) be the group of n × n unitary matrices, and O(n) be the group of n × n orthogonal matrices.
Let SU(n) = {A ∈U(n)| det A = 1} be the special unitary group and SO(n) = {A ∈O(n)| det A = 1}
be the special orthogonal group. All U(n), SU(n), O(n), SO(n) are Lie groups with natural manifold
structures.
(a) Compute the dimensions of SU(n) and SO(n).
(b) Compute the fundamental groups of SU(n) and SO(n) (n ⩾2).
5) Let (M, g) be a compact Riemannian manifold and R be its Riemannian curvature tensor. (M, g) will
be called weakly negative if for any point p ∈M and for any nonzero vector ﬁeld X ∈TpM, there
exists a nonzero vector ﬁeld Y ∈TpM such that R(X, Y, Y, X) < 0.
(a) Let X be a Killing vector ﬁeld and f = 1
2g(X, X) = 1
2|X|2. Show that for any vector ﬁeld V
(Hessf) (V, V ) = g(∇V X, ∇V X) −R(V, X, X, V ).
Here the Hessian of f is (Hessf) (Y, Z) := g(∇Y grad(f), Z) for any vector ﬁelds Y, Z, where
grad(f) is the gradient vector of f.
(b) Prove that if (M, g) is weakly negative, then there are no nontrivial Killing vector ﬁelds.
