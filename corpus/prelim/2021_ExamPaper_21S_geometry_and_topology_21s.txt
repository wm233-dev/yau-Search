S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contests 2021
Geometry and Topology
Solve every problem.
Problem 1.
(a) Show that P2n can not be the boundary of a compact manifold.
(b) Show that P3 is the boundary of some compact manifold.
Problem 2. Suppose M is a noncompact, complete n-dimensional manifold, and suppose there is an open
subset U ⊂M and an open set V ⊂Rn such that M\U is isomorphic to Rn\V. If RicM ≥0, show that M is
isometric to Rn.
Problem 3. Compute all the homotopy groups of the n-torus T n = S1 × S1 × · · · × S1, n ≥2.
Problem 4.
Consider the upper half space H3 = {(x, y, z) | z > 0} equipped with hyperbolic metric
g = dx2 + dy2 + dz2
z2

2 ). Compute

Problem 5. Suppose M is a compact 2-dimensional Riemannian manifold without boundary, with positive
sectional curvature. Show that any two compact closed geodesics on M must intersect with each other.
Problem 6.
Suppose Σ is a smooth compact embedded hypersurface (i.e. a codimension 1 submanifold)
without boundary in Rn for n ≥3. Show that Σ is orientable.
