Problem 3. Let n ≥1 be a positive integer, and let U(n) be the group of unitary
matrices in Cn.
(i) Compute π2(U(n)) and π3(U(n)).
(ii) Prove that the determinant map det : U(n) →S1 induces an isomorphism on
fundamental groups.
